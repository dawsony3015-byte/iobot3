--HALLOWEEN COSTUMES: We look forward to seeing your creativity this Halloween! As you plan, help us
make the day fun and welcoming for everyone. Choose a character, not a cultural stereotype. You can dress
as a character you admire without changing your skin color, imitating an accent, or making someone’s culture or
identity the joke. Avoid sacred or ceremonial clothing and costumes that make light of disabilities, violence, or
real suffering. Keep your face visible and leave all weapons and weapon props at home. Your costume
should also allow you to participate in the school day. Before choosing, ask yourself: Could someone feel
mocked, excluded, or unsafe because of my costume? Even when we mean well, our choices can affect others.
Dressing up is optional—everyone belongs, with or without a costume. If you have a question, please check with
a Dean before Halloween. We’re happy to help you think it through.
